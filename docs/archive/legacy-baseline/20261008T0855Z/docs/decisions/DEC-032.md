# DEC-032 — Minimal V1 Money Policy

Status: APPROVED

Decision Authority: Phan Đoàn Thế Quân — Solo Owner of StyleMind

Date: 2026-09-21

Source Decision Review: DR-007

Resolved OPEN Questions: OPEN-012

## Approved decision

StyleMind V1 uses VND only. Monetary values are whole Vietnamese đồng with zero decimal places; fractional VND is not exposed to users.

The backend is authoritative for Product/Variant price, Cart total, Checkout total and Payment amount. The frontend must not calculate the authoritative payable amount.

V1 pricing scope:

1. Shipping fee is 0 VND and is not charged.
2. StyleMind V1 introduces no separate tax/VAT calculation line. Catalog item price is the product price used by the application; this is not a statement about external legal or tax obligations.
3. Discounts and promotions are out of scope unless introduced by a later explicit approved decision.

Refund basis:

1. Refund is calculated from the amount actually paid for eligible item quantities.
2. For a partial item return, refund equals paid item unit amount multiplied by approved returned quantity.
3. Cumulative refunds must not exceed the amount actually paid for the eligible Order/payment.
4. No separate shipping, application-level discount or tax component requires refund allocation in V1.
5. Refund eligibility and timing follow DEC-034.
