## Approved decision

Option A from the DR-001 review is approved. StyleMind has one final decision maker for product/business and technical/architecture decisions:

- Product Owner / Business Approver: Phan Đoàn Thế Quân
- Backend/Microservices reviewer: Phan Đoàn Thế Quân
- Frontend/UX reviewer: Phan Đoàn Thế Quân
- AI/Recommendation reviewer: Phan Đoàn Thế Quân
- Architecture reviewer: Phan Đoàn Thế Quân

The approved success baseline is:

1. Commerce correctness follows Product → Variant → Cart → Checkout → Payment → Order.
2. Stylist usefulness connects User Context + Wardrobe + Catalog + Outfit Recommendation + Commerce.
3. Every product presented by AI as purchasable during acceptance testing must resolve to a real StyleMind Catalog Product/Variant entity.
4. Hard constraints must be obeyed; soft preferences are evaluated for relevance.

The current KPI policy uses technical/functional acceptance-level Pass/Fail metrics. Business outcome metrics such as conversion, retention, revenue, satisfaction or arbitrary AI-accuracy targets remain TBD and are not invented by this decision.

## Scope implications

This decision establishes the approval authority and the acceptance framing for the BRD and later specifications. It does not approve a business KPI value, a model/provider, an architecture boundary or an implementation mechanism.

- Detailed AI evaluation datasets, rubrics and runtime constraints remain subject to the later specification workflow.
- All unrelated OPEN questions and the fourteen deferred decision clusters remain unchanged.
