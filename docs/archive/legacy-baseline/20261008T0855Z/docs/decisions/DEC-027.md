# DEC-027 — Target Segment and First Milestone Boundary

Status: APPROVED

Decision Authority: Phan Đoàn Thế Quân — Solo Developer & Product Owner

Date: 2026-09-21

Source Decision Review: DR-002

Resolved OPEN Questions: OPEN-002, OPEN-003

## Approved decision

StyleMind's V1 target is female customers within a women-first product direction that supports diverse fashion styles and is not restricted to one predefined aesthetic.

StyleMind is a complete solo-developed personal project and is not constrained to a tiny short-term demo. This does not automatically place every previously discussed idea into V1.

The approved V1 core is:

1. Commerce Core: Product/Variant Catalog, Cart, Checkout, Payment and Order.
2. Smart Wardrobe: store and manage items the user already owns.
3. AI Stylist: compose outfits using Wardrobe and real Catalog data, with 100% grounding for purchasable recommendations.

## Scope implications

The V1 business boundary includes the commerce lifecycle, a usable Smart Wardrobe capability and an AI Stylist connected to real catalog products. The broader StyleMind direction remains preserved, but advanced or future concepts do not enter V1 merely because they appeared in discovery material.

## Explicit exclusions and deferred items

- Women-first does not define one fixed age range or one exclusive aesthetic.
- Advanced/future concepts not listed in the approved V1 core are not automatically approved for V1.
- Detailed capability acceptance and later milestone placement remain governed by the relevant approved or deferred decisions.
- This decision does not select architecture, service boundaries, APIs, database structures or AI providers.
