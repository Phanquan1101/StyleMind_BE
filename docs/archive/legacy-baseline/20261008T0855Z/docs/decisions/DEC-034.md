# DEC-034 — Conservative V1 Cancellation, Return and Refund Policy

Status: APPROVED

Decision Authority: Phan Đoàn Thế Quân — Solo Owner of StyleMind

Source Decision Review: DR-009

Resolved OPEN Questions: OPEN-026

## Approved decision

Cancellation:

1. Customer cancellation is allowed only in PENDING, PAYMENT_PENDING, PAID or CONFIRMED.
2. Cancellation is not allowed in PROCESSING, SHIPPED, COMPLETED, CANCELLED, EXPIRED or FAILED.
3. V1 supports whole-Order cancellation only; item-level cancellation is out of scope.
4. A cancellation reason is mandatory.
5. Unpaid PENDING/PAYMENT_PENDING cancellation may proceed without refund.
6. PAID/CONFIRMED cancellation requires review. Approved cancellation creates refund eligibility for the valid paid amount; rejected cancellation includes a visible reason.

Return:

1. Return is allowed only after the Order reaches COMPLETED.
2. The request window is 7 calendar days from the time the Order becomes COMPLETED.
3. Item-level, partial-quantity and full returns are supported.
4. Exchange/replacement is out of scope for V1.
5. A return request requires Order, Item, quantity, reason and description. Evidence is optional at submission; admin may require or review available evidence.
6. Admin review and QC are required before a physical returned item is finally accepted for refund.
7. Invalid ownership/state/window/quantity or failure to satisfy approved return/QC conditions may cause rejection.

Refund:

1. Refund becomes eligible after approved cancellation of a paid Order or after an approved Return completes required receipt/QC processing.
2. Refund follows DEC-032 and cannot exceed the valid amount actually paid for eligible items/quantities.
3. SePay/VietQR refunds use the approved admin refund process to a verified refund destination.
4. COD refunds use a user-provided and verified bank refund destination.
5. V1 does not promise fully automatic payout/refund execution; admin-controlled/manual processing is allowed.
6. Refund states may include REFUND_PENDING, PROCESSING, COMPLETED and FAILED. A failed refund requires admin attention and may be retried after the cause is addressed without creating additional refund amount.
