## Approved decision

Frontend or user action must never directly mark an online payment as PAID.

For SePay/VietQR:

1. Payment is successful only after valid authoritative confirmation can be matched to the intended payment/Order and matches the exact required amount.
2. Duplicate confirmation/webhook events must not repeat business effects.
3. User-provided “I already paid” evidence is out of scope for V1.
4. The payment attempt expires 15 minutes after the payment transaction/QR is generated.
5. An expired attempt cannot be revived. If the Order remains eligible, the user may initiate a new payment attempt.
6. Underpayment, overpayment and unmatched payment do not mark the Order as paid and go to exception/admin handling.
7. Late payment after payment/Order expiry or cancellation must not resurrect the Order or move a terminal Order to an active state. It becomes an exception for reconciliation; any required refund follows DEC-034.
8. Payment versus expiry/cancellation races are resolved only by authoritative backend processing, with no automatic revival of a terminal Order.

For COD:

1. COD does not mean the Order is financially PAID at Order creation.
2. A COD Order may progress according to the approved Order lifecycle while Payment remains unpaid or pending collection.
3. COD becomes PAID only when collection is actually confirmed.
4. Failed delivery or failure to collect does not mark Payment as paid; the Order follows the applicable failed/cancelled delivery lifecycle.
5. Exact carrier integration and technical COD reconciliation remain deferred.
