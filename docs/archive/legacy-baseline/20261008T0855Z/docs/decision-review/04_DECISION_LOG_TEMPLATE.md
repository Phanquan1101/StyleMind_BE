## Proposed completion rules

PROPOSED — HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED. Keep all existing IDs stable. When a record is actually needed, allocate the next unused DEC identifier through the approved registry process; check the live registry first. Do not allocate one merely to hold a suggestion in this package.

An APPROVED record must identify the approving person/role, exact chosen behavior or scope, rationale, affected IDs and consequences. A referenced DR package may contain several OPEN records and several clauses per record. List an OPEN under Resolved only when its full question is answered; otherwise record the answered clause and remaining detail in Notes/Deferred details. Approval of an evaluation approach, for example, does not answer provider choice, all thresholds or the full outfit contract.

For a DEFERRED item, name the responsible human, the later artifact/decision trigger, the detail still unresolved and the limitations on work that depends on it. Deferral is not a product answer. Rejection of an option does not silently approve a competing option.

For supersession, cite exact old DEC and affected requirement/rule IDs, the changed assertions, the replacement record and the approving authority. Retain the old record and source evidence. Dates alone do not establish precedence. If an approved choice changes scope or a confirmed constraint, record explicit supersession rather than editing the old text invisibly.

No fields above are filled from an agent recommendation. Detailed approval authority and record lifecycle require approval of [baseline governance](05_BASELINE_GOVERNANCE.md).
